# Feature Status

This list contains the canonical 20 features for Roamwise.

1. Destination photos, hero images, and place galleries
2. Interactive map showing destinations, hotels, and day routes
3. Shareable trip links for group members
4. Invite members by email or share code
5. Comments and voting on itinerary items
6. Drag-and-drop itinerary ordering
7. Calendar export (.ics) for the full trip
8. Downloadable/printable itinerary PDF
9. Weather forecast and packing suggestions for travel dates
10. Local transport estimates between itinerary stops
11. Live budget progress with category breakdown charts
12. Expense entry and settlement tracking between travelers
13. Saved/favorite destinations and places
14. Recently viewed trips and destinations
15. Trip templates: weekend, honeymoon, family, solo, backpacking
16. Better destination fallback: suggest close matches and explain unavailable destinations
17. Admin data-review panel to verify places, hours, prices, and sources
18. Accessibility filters: wheelchair-friendly, low walking, senior-friendly, child-friendly
19. Offline-friendly trip view / installable PWA support
20. Dashboard improvements: trip cards, progress, countdown, upcoming trip highlights
